What Laundrix is being built to manage

Customers and orders

Find customer details and order history. Price laundry by load, kilogram or item.

Payments and invoices

Track what’s paid and what’s owing, and combine outstanding services on one invoice.

Point of sale

Record counter sales for drinks, snacks and other retail products.

Stock and inventory

Track quantities, deduct stock when products are sold and see low-stock alerts.

Business reports

Review orders and payments in daily, monthly and yearly reports.

Staff and branding

Give employees individual accounts, track activity and customise themes and invoice layouts.

Frequently asked questions

Can I sign up for Laundrix?

Public sign-ups are currently closed while the private pilot is being developed. You can try the interactive demo with fictional records.

How long is the free trial?

The free trial will last 15 days once public sign-ups open. You can explore the demo today.

Can I charge per kilogram or per load?

Yes. The pilot is designed for pricing by kilogram, load or item. Kilogram quantities can include decimals.

Is Laundrix for collecting my personal laundry?

Laundrix is software for laundry businesses to manage their own customers and orders.

How to put Laundrix on your phone

Open this page on the phone you want to use.

iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen. If you see Open as Web App, turn it on.
  4. Tap Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top.
  3. Tap Add to home screen.
  4. Tap Install or Add, whichever you see.

Go to your phone’s home screen. Tap the Laundrix Owner icon. Sign in with your owner email and password.

You can also sign in here in your browser whenever you like.

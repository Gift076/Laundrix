About this prototype

This is an early browser-based prototype. It stores demo data in this browser only. It is not yet a live multi-tenant subscription service and should not be used for real customer records until secure cloud accounts, backups, access controls and payment processing are implemented.

Next development milestone: secure online database, separate business accounts, subscription billing, receipt printing and tested scale integration.
